# Human Review Checklist

Before publishing, a human reviewer should confirm:

- The report answers the original question.
- The selected report profile matches the user's intent.
- The executive summary is understandable in under 2 minutes.
- Major factual claims have citations.
- Important sources are current enough for the topic.
- The evidence matrix separates verified, weak, disputed, outdated, and
  unverified claims.
- The analysis includes tradeoffs and uncertainty, not only benefits.
- Recommendations are practical and proportionate to the evidence.
- Limitations and assumptions are visible.
- No confidential, unsafe, or unsupported content is included.

Profile-specific checks:

- Technical reports: code, diagrams, and implementation claims are correct or
  clearly labeled as pseudocode/conceptual.
- Market reports: market-size and competitor claims have dated sources and clear
  assumptions.
- Policy reports: stakeholder impacts, legal/regulatory context, and uncertainty
  are visible.
- Literature reviews: search method, inclusion/exclusion criteria, disagreements,
  and evidence gaps are visible.
