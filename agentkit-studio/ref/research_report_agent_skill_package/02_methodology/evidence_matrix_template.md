# Evidence Matrix Template

```markdown
| ID | Claim | Source | Source Type | Date | Reliability 1-5 | Relevance 1-5 | Status | Used In | Quote | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | <claim supported by the source> | <title or organization> | <academic/official/news/docs/vendor/etc.> | <date or unknown> | 1-5 | 1-5 | verified | <section> | <short verified quote if available> | <caveats/conflicts> |
```

## Evidence Fields

Each evidence item should include:

- `id`
- `claim`
- `source`
- `source_type`
- `url`
- `date`
- `reliability`
- `relevance`
- `status`
- `used_in`
- `quote`
- `notes`

## Status Values

- `verified`
- `partially_supported`
- `disputed`
- `outdated`
- `unverified`
- `weak`

## Profile Notes

- Technical reports may include docs, source repositories, standards, and papers.
- Market reports may include company filings, analyst reports, surveys, pricing
  pages, and reputable news.
- Policy reports may include laws, regulations, government reports, NGOs,
  academic work, and stakeholder statements.
- Literature reviews should emphasize search method, publication venue, and
  evidence quality.

Unverified evidence may be recorded in the matrix but must not support a key
finding or recommendation.
